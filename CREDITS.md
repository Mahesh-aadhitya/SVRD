## `public/images/deity-hero.png`

- Source: devotee/temple photo of the garlanded Sri Varadaraja Swamy utsava murti, provided directly by the user.
- Changes: cropped to the deity and its own garlands/shoulder ornaments (excluding the separate flanking flower panels and offering bed visible in the original photo), corners feathered to transparent with a soft rounded-rectangle alpha mask so it sits on the app's background without a hard rectangular photo edge, while keeping the garlands and hands fully opaque. Automatic AI background removal (rembg, u2net and isnet-general-use models) was tried first but produced broken cutouts on this densely garlanded photo, so a feathered crop was used instead.
- Confirm the temple's rights to this specific photo before public launch.

## `public/images/makara-thoranam.png`

- Source: a gold makara-thoranam (temple archway) product photo, provided directly by the user.
- Changes: white studio background removed via a color-distance threshold (the background was a clean, near-uniform white, unlike the deity photo above, so this cut cleanly).
- Confirm rights to this specific photo before public launch.

## `public/images/shankha-watermark.png` / `chakra-watermark.png`

- Source: black-and-white line-art illustrations of a shankha and a chakra, provided directly by the user.
- Changes: converted to transparent-background art tinted maroon (luminosity used as the alpha mask, so the white paper became transparent and the ink lines became the maroon shape), trimmed to the artwork's bounding box.

## `public/images/placeholder-*.svg`

- Generated by `scripts/gen-placeholders.mjs`: simple gradient tiles with a generic Vaishnava symbol (lotus, diya, shankha, chakra), used only as stand-ins for pooja/event/gallery content until real temple photos are uploaded through the admin panel. Not meant to be permanent.

Any redistribution of this app's assets should keep this file updated and confirm rights for the source images above before a public launch.
